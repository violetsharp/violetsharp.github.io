01 / A LITTLE ABOUT MEBEHIND THE CAMERA, AND SOMETIMES IN FRONT OF THE PAGE

I want to make things that feel like being understood.

I'm a filmmaker first, and a writer whenever the images need somewhere else to go.

I’m drawn to intimate moments, complicated people, beautiful imperfections, and the feeling a scene leaves behind after it ends. I love cinema as a way of paying attention: to light, silence, a look held half a second too long, or the distance between what someone says and what they mean.

This site isn't a persona or a performance. It's my own little corner of the internet — a place to share what I'm making, what I'm learning, and the stories I haven't quite let go of yet.

— Claire
