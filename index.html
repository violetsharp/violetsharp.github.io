01 / POSITIONTHE CAMERA IS NOT INNOCENT

Not a mask.
A point of view.

Violet is an entity behind the camera, inside the sentence, and somewhere outside the frame.

Filmmaking and writing are ways of looking closer: at alienation, desire, power, intimacy, and the stories people tell to survive themselves. Sometimes the image lies. Sometimes the narrator does. Sometimes the truth is only the cut between them.

This archive is not a neat biography. It is a record of contradictions: tenderness beside cruelty, conviction beside doubt, absurdity beside the unbearable.

04 / SUBJECT FILEIDENTITY IS NOT A CLOSED CASE

FILE STATUS: UNRESOLVED

An entity.
Not an answer.

Violet exists in the gap between how the world names someone and how that someone experiences being alive. Alienation is not proof of inhumanity; it is often evidence of the world's refusal to recognise a person on their own terms.

Desire, sexuality, tenderness, anger, politics, and absurdity belong here without needing to become a clean moral lesson. The narrator may be a villain in one light and a hero in another. Neither label tells the whole story.
