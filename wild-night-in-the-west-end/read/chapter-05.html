Love Is a Verb

The manuscript text is presented as supplied, with formatting for comfortable reading.

Fuckin' aye, it's Davina again, you might be wondering what am I doing now and well, I'm just trying to notice the why trip-hop is coming back, what a great genre, you know what's great though? Nathaniel Merriweather, I mean, To Catch A Thief is a song of all time, alongside When I'm Small, perhaps, I should invest in a driver's license, go for a late night drive in London, get in a tunnel and listen to Massive Attack and Phantogram all night.
Jokes aside, I'm really really bored right now, I just had lunch, a jacket potato with tuna and mayo and some baked beans and cheese, I like to try weird combos of food, also, as iconic as I am, I drank some red wine alongside it because that's just who I am I guess?
I just can't wait for nighttime, I'll go to a club and likely get an hookup since it's Friday night and I am bored out of my mind from doing my job as a web designer, shit is boring nowadays and terrible because AI is taking over in my area so I don't get contracts anywhere so it's mostly freelancing on that and working at a local fancy restaurant, crazy I swear, at least I get paid well and treated well which is great truly, I'm sick of toxic environment's.
I'm planning a trip to France also, I'm likely going to Paris, I'm keen to get some YSL clothes though since I saw god at a bathroom stall and asked him to forget my sins and pray to Saint Laurent, music references, ignore them.
I could also go to Berlin to the Berghain or Panorama Bar, if I do so, it'll be on a plane or a dark and long train.
Night time means, alcohol, a tiny bit not much, sex, lot's of it, a whole night, being delusional, all the time and staring at the window and trying to change the hookup habit and chase a relationship with an actual person and not just sex, must feel great, the tenderness, the warmth, the cuddling, the kissing, it all might feel much better than a random hookup.
I should stop the ranting about being bored, It's getting boring, I apologize.
I should acknowledge other stuff though, I've heard there's a girl that frequently talks about my looks, my curly purple hair, my nice tits, my you only live once look to it, apparently she has pink hair, shows a lot of whimsy, the complete opposite of me, but, some would debate, opposites do attract.
Perhaps, I should talk to her, in the end of the day Love Is a Verb, but also, I still have this sort of trauma from my ex leaving me for another girl.
I don't know, perhaps I'm a mess.
