Chapter 1
Crowded

The manuscript text is presented as supplied, with formatting for comfortable reading.

Friday, 2nd of October 2026, the streets are crowded, there's a performance in the West End Theater, "The Way It Goes", It's rather interesting since last I heard, It's a piece that is very philosophical and confusing to understand at first, therefore, most people watch 2 times, I'll be one of them since I'm rather interested in it, bought my ticket the other day for 3 quid which is rather cheap considering It's The West fucking End and It's a 3 hour and 33 minute piece, so I'm guessing it might be an epic or anything, also, this is between us, my name is Elias, I'm a retired photographer and haven't properly introduced myself, I'm 34 year's old, which is rather young to be "retired", but, I'll explain.
I am retired mostly due to the fact that I broke my film camera, and yes, you could argue there's just digital camera's nowadays but, personally, I just prefer shooting on 120mm on the camera my mom gave me as a kid, she was a photographer herself, she mostly shot on Piccadilly, but, since she was a big traveler, she also shot a bunch of times on the Times Square in New York and also on the landscapes in the Swiss Alps, what a fucking dream really, I wish I got that luck myself, but I ended up only moving between Glasgow, Berlin and Cologne, which aren't bad but it's not as interesting as visiting for example, Dublin and learn more about Irish culture, visit the Scottish landscapes and feel like James Bond, but no, I ended up in London after all, this is absolute shite, bunch of morons everywhere.
Either way, I have to go to the theater friend, see you later.
