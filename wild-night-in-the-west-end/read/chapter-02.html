How I love sipping Rebina on a Friday night while everyone's outside trying to go to the West End Theatre.
What a load Einstein must've had, a bunch of moron's everywhere, why watch a 3 hour piece that likely will make you fall asleep after one hour, oh and by the way, I'm Davina nice to meet you, I'm 20 and glad of it, I'm from Scotland and sadly didn't get into Oxford, but not everything's so bad when you get to live your dream life in one of the more interesting cities in the world, like sure, you can go to the Times Square in New York but everything there is just flatter and more perfect compared to Glasgow, I love Glasgow due to it's imperfections and it's architecture which unlike fucking USA doesn't have a boring look to it, you get to see red bricked houses everywhere, while in the US you get to see an orange looking moron commenting some bullshit everywhere and always talking about how they have to make America great again but let's face it, America wouldn't be shit without the British and nonetheless is it relevant to most of the world since most see it as a joke of a country.
Anyway, if you wanna know what my dream life is well, hook ups at Friday night, sipping on Rebina, fucking a Coca-Cola, waking up with a sunrise on my face which I'll mention, is highly unlikely but still nice when it happens, I love cloudy and rainy weather, hook ups and other business feel's much better then, for example, writing, I prefer clear sky night's when I get to see Betelgeuse and Bellatrix, I get to make some sapphic delusions in my head of me and my future wife or husband, for an husband though, I'd probably stare at the moon.
My Moon My Man.
Another nightclub and my hand on my chest hoping I'll find the one someday, my wife, my husband, the Betelgeuse to my Bellatrix, I just want to tell them and what they want me to be and I'll be their Pokémon.
Sometimes it's a regular a day, or a regular night but the sky is looking so lovely but I'm just staring at you, it's hard not to when you're giving Jennifer's Body, therefore, I'll be crazy and needy for you.
