Chapter 3
Marianne

The manuscript text is presented as supplied, with formatting for comfortable reading.

Fuckin' Aye, another morning in Westminster, London. I hate this place, It's so fucking shite, focáil leis an áit shalach seo.
You looking moron, let me introduce myself, I'm Marianne, born and raised in Ireland, proud of being Irish, but due to a shite decision made by my parent's when I was 13, I ended up in this place, focála London, out of every goddamn place on Earth we go to the same one who committed a genocide against us and said it was only a famine, what a shite feckin' place.
Just a fun fact, I'm Irish and Half-Scottish, crazy right? How come my parents were such morons to come to this place and even die on it and leaving me alone here when I was just 19, at least they gave their will to me and not the fucking British aye.
I'll never understand their fascination with London, it's just such a mediocre place compared to my hometown Dingle, what a beautiful fucking place aye.
Anyhow, let me tell you a bit of what I do, occasionally I get bored so I make music in my tiny apartment bedroom, sadly enough, my neighbours are arseholes and are always complaining when I either play my synthesizer or just sing really, somehow he hears anything, sadly enough I don't have money to rent a studio or to buy one, therefore I just have an old laptop, a cheap interface, some okay headphones and an overkill synthesizer.
Feck how can this get any worse I swear, my shit is tape machines, old electromagnetic keyboards and vintage drums but no, I'm stuck with an overkill synthesizer and no drum machines at all, just the ones of me beating the hell out of my desk but anyway, I also love to just go out at night and talk to random people on the street really and work on my social anxiety since nothing can solve my OCD and autism, my dumbass parents though, believed I could be cured from being gay so yeah, world's a crazy place and if I met a man everything would make sense again, my dream in life, oh my god put it in me please pleas... FUCK that, I rather be whimsical and mysterious and have a bleached and pinkish hair and wear cute and silly skirts and call myself Bellatrix since my full government name is Marianne Isabella Fraser, I might hate my parents for a bunch of shite but my name isn't one of them and my freedom isn't also.
