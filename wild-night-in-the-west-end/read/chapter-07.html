Chapter 7
First Interactions

The manuscript text is presented as supplied, with formatting for comfortable reading.

Elias humming

I'm trapped in this body I can't, get out!

a young lady goes against Elias and says

Oh I'm so sorry sir!

Elias replies slightly blushing

Sir? Are you calling me old?

young lady replies

THATS NOT WHAT I MEA..!

Elias interrupts

EASY THERE I'M KIDDING!
I'm Elias nice to meet you!

young lady replies

Oh! I'm Adrianne nice to meet you!

Elias replies

Nice to meet you Adrianne.
You look stunning!

Adrianne replies

Oh! So do you Elias!
What do you do for a living?
I mean why would one wear such a sharp suit on a Friday night?

Elias replies

I'm a retired photographer, what about you?

Adrianne replies

Aren't you quite young to be retired?

Elias replies while chuckling

Haha! Yeah I am quite young to be retired, BUT, it's a long story!
I still make some money freelancing around restaurants, investments, design and I guess just being a clown in the middle of the street?

Adrianne replies

AH I see, now I see where that charming and quite funny tone comes from!

Elias replies

Actually comes from my mom actually, she was a huge traveler!

Adrianne replies

Then you must be the same no?

Elias replies

Not really no, I moved between Glasgow, Berlin and Cologne but just ended up in this shithole called London, could be worse though!

Adrianne replies

Yes it could!
Also, I found you sort of handsome, can I get your number?

Elias replies

Sure, there you go!
We can set up a date tomorrow if you want to, I'm now going to watch the piece at the West End Theatre!

Adrianne replies surprised

Oh really?
I'm part of the cast!

Elias surprised

Oh shit really?

Adrianne replies

Yeah I am!
Perhaps we could hangout after the piece and have a pint together!

Elias awkwardly

Well huh! Sure thing!?
See you later!

Adrianne replies

See you later, I'll leave a word to security to let you through the backstage!
