Chapter 4
Die Schriftstellerin

The manuscript text is presented as supplied, with formatting for comfortable reading.

So, just another day in the West End, crowded as hell but I get to eat bangers and mash at a fine restaurant and have some beer alongside it, what a fucking dream aye?
Well you might be asking, who are you? You won't know it yet, I could tell you but I could give you a thousand responses all false, because truth is I'm a rather controversial and bad person trying to change, choose life they said in Trainspotting, that's the exact same philosophy I'll chase rather than spending my life doing absolutely nothing or alternatively I could just say carpe-fucking-diem and actually start doing something already, but I'd rather not since I'm too busy with stuff like politics and questioning everyone's morals for whatever reason, so in the meanwhile, we could just dance to Boogie Wonderland or cry to Jigsaw Falling Into Place because why the hell not, goddamn perfect songs for real.
I could also just have a quick drink and appreciate the great outdoors, but why would I do that also, like for real, what would be the point, inspiration, enjoyment, appreciating the colors of life?
I rather enjoy music, music and film is my passion, One Battle After Another, Once Upon A Time In Hollywood, Heat, Baby Driver, absolute peak. Then there's music Radiohead, Clairo, Childish Gambino, Elysian Fields, beabadoobee, I love feeling something due to music and film I swear, it's refreshing.
Anyhow, I'm not saying anything interesting now because I have no thoughts inside my head so uhh, see you later I guess?
