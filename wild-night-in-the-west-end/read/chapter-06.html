Chapter 6 - Part I - Presentation

incomprehensible audience noise
shouting

AND HELLO LADIES AND GENTLEMEN AND WELCOME TO ANOTHER FRIDAY NIGHT IN THE WEST END THEATRE WHERE ANYTHING CAN HAPPEN!
First of all, if you know me you already know my name, BUT, if you're new here, my name is David but you can call me Dave!
I've been working here for a few years now and already have presented pieces like The Ballads Of Murder and the Burning Desires Of Jane, rather a long title, BUT, it's worth watching it! Now, for the first time EVER in this theatre, there's a really popular piece finally being played on here, it goes by The Way Things Go and it's rather a deeply philosophical, metaphorical and....

someone in the audience

SHUT THE FUCK UP!

Dave replies

Oh wow, people are rude aren't they?

audience laughs
Dave continues

As I was saying, The Way Things Go is one of those pieces where you question a lot of things about life, grief, dreams, religion, politics, everything, now, this might be a first time, BUT, since according to most people it needs to be watched for at least 2 times, next Friday night we will be hosting this piece again with same casting, but, tickets will be more expensive due to Kinnerent Entertainment, the company that publishes the piece, instead of the 3 pounds price tag it'll be 7 pounds which is quite unfortunate.
Anyway, It's almost 10pm, SO, LADIES AND GENTLEMENT, GET READY FOR THE WAY THINGS GO!
