PART I / THE VOID

Violet

Chapter 1 — The Traveller

It all started a year ago, I decided to move out of my parents place and with that I felt, lost.

I didn’t know what to do, if I’d get a job or just become anything like a drummer or a singer or whatever, truly.

Politics also stressed me out a lot since political nihilism is on the rise and so was society ignorance towards art and trying to replace it with AI generated bullshit that lacked everything but primarily, a soul, from there I knew we were doomed, in fact I knew that since I knew about Oppenheimer and his creation, the atomic bomb.

He basically gave us a power to destroy ourselves and many governments and militaries got seduced by the idea pretty much because they thought it would bring peace, but well as we all know we only exist due to a Soviet called Stanislav Petrov that pretty much prevented a potential nuclear war back in 1983 by identifying a false alarm from a Soviet warning system.

Well, back then we had 2 main extreme ideologies that last until today, fascism and communism, which well, on one hand you let the 1% become much much richer and you will judge minorities, immigration and tell obvious lies that make an excellent propaganda that if you speak against it they’ll get to you and take away everything like in communism, where well.

Everyone starves and you don’t have a state but their torture method that similarly compares to fascism but they can somehow be much worse, they’ll take away your existence pretty much, they’ll take away your family citizenship alongside yours and they might even kill you in front of them, but well, in the end of the day they’re both dictatorships that don’t work.

Done with this political nonsense and I’d like to address my life that is a complete mess, well, I have no name in this city, they just call me The Traveller, my mental health well, its also not the best and I feel like shit all the time, I’m pretty much a modern day Fernando Pessoa, a Portuguese writer who had multiple so called heteronyms and whose last name translated to English is basically “person”.

Also he pretty much be considered the man who never lived.

My days are pretty much a mess and I’ll either stay home all day thinking about killing myself or how insignificant we are compared to the whole universe, like, what do you mean the Milky Way is just a void in middle of space and that we just might as well be completely alone on it, with no other living beings and just surrounded by a whole void in space.

I know I also sound like pretty much a modern day nihilist or existentialist and honestly I just won’t deny it due to the fact that I am and yes I just think that we shouldn’t exist by nature’s law and I also just think that nothing truly matters in the end of the day, we are nothing but numbers or just well, I don’t know, accidents?

Chapter 2 — A Quiet Life

For a while, I decided that taking a quiet life would be a better option but I actually found myself in a pretty purple world and well, in case you don’t understand I’ll explain it to you, I basically see a purple world as disorienting in a lot of ways so, what I’m basically saying is I’m completely disoriented in this city and in my head.

I’m sick of trying to search for a job which well I shouldn’t according to society’s standard bullshit but in all honesty I just see myself becoming either a writer which I am being right now or an actor in small movies since many people told me I have the talent for it but I don’t know.

I don’t even know why I’m searching for a job since I don’t give a shit about money pretty much or anything except for arts or something related to such and as I mentioned before art is dying due to the rise of AI that just copies other people’s work and doesn’t make anything better from it just empty and soulless bullshit.

Back when I was a kid they’d read me Romeo & Juliet and other stories that were made totally by human hand, for example, some Portuguese works from Fernando Pessoa whom I mentioned before and many other Portuguese ones like Luís Vaz De Camões and then other European ones most precisely from the United Kingdom and Czech Republic like George Orwell whose actual name was Eric Arthur Blair and Franz Kafka that wrote masterpieces like The Trial and Metamorphosis while Orwell wrote Animal Farm and 1984 that looking back weren’t so wrong.

One thing that Fernando Pessoa and Luís Vaz De Camões shared with each other was that they died poor and with addictions like alcoholism and well, from that on I’d say people were just becoming ignorant when Pessoa’s books got published with his heteronyms and they wouldn’t either buy them or read them so yeah we should’ve known in those moments our society was doomed.

Chapter 3 — Lost

Well, as I was in the way of searching for a purpose I’d say I felt lost and I started taking some bullshit just to be able to be awake for the day while at the same time coping with how messier the mess had become and well I started cutting my wrists and paint my walls with my blood and pretty much just started taking at least 5 energy drinks per day and also drugs like LSD and Xanax and hoping I’d die from an atrocious and painful overdose, I started getting nicknamed by the people around me as the paranoid android which made sense since I was doing anything but trying to be better, I had become more self-destructive each day and when I had an atrocious overdose I didn’t die but I wish I just did so then everything just would be over but I saw myself on the psych ward for a whole month to become better but eventually I just pretended to be better when I just was much much worse compared to others that were there, I met this guy, Paul who had gone absolutely crazy due to his divorce with his wife and the fact that they killed his only children, Laurie and Smith who were just 12 and 17, he witnessed the whole thing and eventually he ended up on the psych ward when he was found by the cops trying to shoot himself, he had even wrote a letter and well it wasn’t worth it at all since he’s still suffering till now and well, I actually felt really bad for the guy but I didn’t know how I could help him since I couldn’t even help myself and if I did try to help him I’d probably end up doing more time than I already was doing but who would care right? It would be for a greater good and a survival of a person.

Chapter 4 — Guns and Knives

After I was released from the psych ward I just couldn’t help but get worse and I stopped taking drugs eventually but had much more narrow cuts in my body which well, people started noticing and they just eventually left me so yeah, fuck it, I’ll take much more drugs and be addicted to much more stuff like alcohol and heroin.

I mean considering I was in Deutschland I knew I could just take how much alcohol I wanted and I could find heroin and weed pretty much everywhere, I eventually stopped taking heroin and started doing meth but well.

I ended up stopping taking such since I already had become numb from taking such and they weren’t doing as much as effect as they did back in the day so I stopped taking them definitely and the max I’d do was smoke some rolling tobacco since I didn’t want to smoke a fucking highway with these stupid branded cigarettes and I decided to just stay more natural and to have less effects on my lungs.

My biggest addiction after that was traveling to forests which felt amazing considering I did it the most on Autumn but then I also had another big addiction that was buying random stuff and consume much much more furniture I didn’t need and meaningless things, with the amount of money I wasted on those I could just buy a whole library and consume much more literature but well.

I was as ignorant as the people who didn’t buy George Orwell, Franz Kafka, Pessoa and Camões books so I pretty much just became the stuff I hated the most and I could also just be considered well I don’t know, an hypocrite.

I ended up buying a gun, tried to shoot myself and it didn’t work cuz somehow the gun jammed and religious people called it a miracle and I ended up doing time on a mental hospital that pretty much could be much worse than the psych ward but in the end of the day no matter how much they tried I’d become much worse in theory.

In reality I found people who I related to which I actually wish I could be with again since after that I still hadn’t found anyone like them since everyone just felt so empty compared to those beautiful and broken souls.

Chapter 5 — Blue Lights and Blue Feelings

When I talk about blue feelings, I mostly talk about sadness and numbness, in this case it was both somehow and I couldn’t ask anything except asking for people to stop the noise so I could get some rest but eventually, I started going outside again to nightclubs that most of the time made me feel the same, I’d always drink a coke in those just to have at least some good taste in my mouth.

Something sweet and not something that was sour like I had all the time.

Even between blue lights I still saw many people dancing and cheering to red books while I’d be there just watching and trying to enjoy the sort of crappy music they just played, shit was not like I thought it would be, I thought it would be like the 70s or 80s nightclubs still but I was dead wrong, they didn’t play bands like Queen, ABBA or the legend himself, David Bowie that made Heroes which well.

I’d say Deutsche people love that song as much as I do since there is nothing like it, genuinely.

There was this time I met a guy in a nightclub and he was very nice and I started hanging out with him and we fell in love with each other eventually but it didn’t last long, well we did things couples do, kiss, cuddle.

Fuck and we also went shopping together but sadly I was a little too much for him so I decided to break up with him and it was hard and I still loved him but I didn’t think he deserved being with a person as unstable as me and he deserved a much better person who didn’t have the same problems as me and as unstable.

The funny thing about all of this is that when I was with him I was actually getting better but at the same time I felt a lot of guilt due to the fact he was very stressed about work and too worried about me to think of work even which well I’d say it’s a sacrifice you do when you’re truly in love with someone but at the same time i’d say that people shouldn’t worry about me at all even my partners.

Personally though, I’d say I’m a terrible partner for some reason.

Chapter 6 — Void

What the fuck is a void?

Depending on your perspective the void can have many colors and forms, it can be black, white, red or it can be a person, a house or a wall, but in my case, it’s where I accidentally travelled to, I travelled to space and found myself lost there and completely alone, not even stars were around me.

Just absolute darkness and emptiness which is crazy to me since our universe is so big but so small at the same time with many empty spaces on it as if many pieces of it are just missing or they’re just not supposed to be there.

It’s scary but at the same time I feel much more comfortable believing that and being here, in all fairness though, I feel like an homesick alien and that I shouldn’t be here, but at the same time, I love this feeling and where I am, it’s as if I’m completely alone in the universe and I’m unique, I don’t see myself as a person, cockroach or whatever.

I see myself as an entity like an idea which according to a wise man, ideas are bulletproof, you may kill a leader but never the idea.

Even in this void, for some reason I feel like sucking a lemon, everything just seems so sour still about all of this, I’m alone and it feels good but at the same time it’s just so sour and I’m thinking I maybe should go back where I came from, because for some time now I noticed a little light in this void and maybe, just maybe, It’s where I belong.

Chapter 7 — Nightclubs

As I went through the little light I noticed something, I was going back to a nightclub that felt weird, they were playing my favorite song and I wasn’t sucking a lemon anymore and I was seeing everything bending in front of me while there were many people staring at me but the weirdest part was that there was this girl that seemed like she was trying to get me back to reality like I had been there for hours completely fucked up and when I came back everyone worried looking faces just disappeared and this girl was smiling, but I quite couldn’t figure out where I was and who that girl was but one thing I was sure, she may or may not have saved me.

While everyone got back to their stuff, I stayed there at the bar eating some Salzstangen that are pretty much salty pretzel sticks while I drank some coke and tried to get myself back together and this girl kept staring at me and I kept staring at her while smiling, like I had found happiness or any feelings at all?

It was very confusing, but after I got myself all back together I decided to chat with her and she told me I had been unconscious in the chair for 3 hours and as she was finishing what she wanted to say people announced dance time and she chose me as her partner for such.

I immediately told her I’d be her partner for dance but I didn’t feel like having a relationship with her but rather a friendship and she was okay with it and after dance time we exchanged numbers with each other and we started hanging out regularly.

For the first time I felt like I had an actual friend and someone I could count on but as always, I ended up ruining everything.

She asked me out and let’s say I sort of freaked out and started being an asshole towards her and I ended up leaving, but doing such I broke both me and her and I was right back at it again, the same damn thing as before, a mess.

Chapter 8 — Colors

Right where I was, the mess, it was different this time, I actually thought everything was in its right place but I was dead wrong, I actually started only having 2 colors in my head now, black and white, the world felt much more off this time than it did before, time seemed like it had slowed down, politics was even messier.

Elections were soon and people kept protesting in the streets for their rights not to be taken and for the 1% to go down.

While people protested and politicians said right things when electioneering, I was just walking past by those like an ignorant and an hypocrite. For some reason I didn’t feel like fighting back against these bullshit politicians and their stupid ideologies but rather just not give a fuck for some reason.

And I call myself an hypocrite due to the fact that I pretty much could get my rights taken away in seconds and everything could blow up at any moment.

At that time I was just focusing on whatever, truly, I just wanted to roam around the streets and try to find something interesting but everything just seemed so blatantly boring that I chose to ignore.

PART II / THE ROAD

The Tourist

Chapter 9 — Ignorance & Politics

It was election time, fascist leaders were on the rise, I finally got an uncontrollable urge to fight back, I promoted the leftist parties that existed at the time alongside some other groups of leftists. It was an absolute chaos coming from the right, they kept throwing hate speech and committing violence towards the immigrants and minorities saying that they were not pure enough and unnatural because the Bible said so, little did they know they were actually going against their own religion saying that nonsensical bullshit, they have been so brainwashed by propaganda that they have become puppets of who they supported and haven’t realized that obvious lies made great propaganda, but as always, the fascists always target the poorly educated.

Election had finished and they were counting the votes for each party, leftist parties were winning at first and people cheered while the right kept attacking until they noticed an enormous rise in votes in right wing parties, more precisely, on the fascist party. Fear spread quickly between social democrats, socialists and communists, while the right cheered.

One day after the election the party was already taking office and making decisions to benefit the 1%.

That’s when everyone realized that their own population had sold the world to the big companies and industries who keep benefiting from modern day slavery, using both children and adults to work on their bullshit products and then being called “successful” when they haven’t done shit but sat in a chair giving orders to anyone that’s below them.

Suicide rates amongst minorities had risen, many immigrants started to be taken somewhere no one knew, some decided to escape the country, some people laughed about it while others cried by watching something they fought for being taken away in nanoseconds of time with just 1 pen and a paper.

I cried and was mess, but I couldn’t help but think about the time I didn’t protest alongside those people and only saw myself as a fucking asshole for letting all of this happen and the only thing I felt was guilt for not doing so, I myself sold the world without the intention of doing so.

Chapter 10 — How To Reinstate Hope

The day has come, the day where we will deny, defend and depose, the day we will be heroes just for one day.

We peacefully started the takeover parade at 1:00am, November 3rd, most of the military went against their bullshit orders that would go against their actual beliefs and helped the people mobilize their trucks and vans, they decided to give cover to the defenseless population who only had uncharged guns with red roses on their barrel.

We waved our flags with the peace symbols while shouting that we could be heroes just for one day.

We reinvented hope for most of the people in our country, everyone cried tears of joy while political prisoners got released and went to see their families, everyone fought using their words while the fascists shot at least 1000 people while they fought for all of this and yet no one who was protesting not even the military shot a bullet to kill anyone, they wanted a peaceful takeover while the shame was on the other side and everyone cheered the fact that they were actually pulling this revolution off.

Some beautiful couples died together due to the fascists, while other couples of any sexuality fought until their last breath, the political prisoners joined the protests still in underwear but no one cared as long as people fought, even in death there was beauty, there was people who fought for hope and whom the fascists called lunatics when the actual lunatics where sitting at their throne with their pride and ego thinking the people wouldn’t win.

We were almost reaching the president stupid throne and the military voluntarily decided to go through it and do the takeover, they arrested the prick and his little friends that have done much harm in the country and in it’s population, all of us together have shown that anyone can be an hero, even if it’s just for one day, you can reinstate hope when you think you can’t, you are more than what you think you are.

We are the people, we rule the world and we will be heroes.

I can remember people standing by the wall and the cops shooting above their heads while some couples kissed and they thought nothing could fall, not even hope.

Chapter 11 — Letters

After reinstating hope alongside the people, I felt the happiest I’ve ever been but after a while I started getting letters.

Those letters were from someone I left, they were telling how much they missed me, but I couldn’t figure out who it was until they finally revealed their name and who they actually were.

It was Valerie, the girl I left due to the fact that she fell in love with me. When she revealed it was her I felt like shit, I was looking back at actions I did for myself and not for others, she said she wishes to speak with me again and I had agreed to it and instead of remembering the shitty old memory of leaving her I was remembering the good ones only and it wasn’t too bad.

We had dinner and we went to a coffee shop, I gave her my new number and even though we didn’t hang out as much as we used to her presence was good enough.

I actually think a lot on what if she had never revealed it was her or what if I never had opened those letters?

Chapter 12 — What If?

After everything that happened I just don’t know how to feel and how to react, everything is so good that I feel like trying to do something good with my life again, I was thinking of helping people, write or whatever really.

But even after all, what if this happiness is all fake? I mean I still don’t have any purpose in life after all of this.

I don’t know what to do and I’m still lost but happy somehow? What if after all I’m just meant to exist?

What’s even the point of doing nothing?

I have no job, I go where I please but I still can’t figure out on why I can keep doing this and it wears me out truly, I wanna be something but I don’t wanna be a number in this little world.

What if after all finding a meaning is just meaningless?

What if after all the old me was right? Nothing in this world truly matters, not even you, not your job, not your money, not who you are, not what you are?

What if we are actually just so irrelevant compared to the universe and we don’t realize it because we don’t want to?

I think it’s happening again, I’m going back to madness.

Chapter 13 — Realization

After the chase of happiness I finally realized, I’m supposed to be a mess, or else I won’t be able to be who I am, I’m the messiest person to ever live, I believe I may deserve an award even if it’s insignificant for me and most.

This realization isn’t bad at all, it finally made me believe in something, reality, truly, some people call stuff that happens to them destiny, I don’t believe in such thing, I believe in reality because it’s what truly is.

Murphy’s Law, anything that can go wrong will go wrong, and that’s okay, it’s reality and we as entities we will accept it, the same way we accepted victory and defeat throughout history, it doesn’t mean good stuff won’t happen, but in the end of the day Murphy’s Law is just the hard truth we have to accept and that’s okay.

I didn’t find myself, I’m still lost, but I found out why everything happens and that’s all I wanted, in reality we are not irrelevant or doomed to anything, things will just happen and we as people we will accept it and then find a better solution to everything, we are scary, we are infinite and I am a traveller.
