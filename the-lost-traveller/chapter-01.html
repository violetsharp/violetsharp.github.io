Chapter 1 — The Traveller

Read slowly. Use the chapter controls below to continue or return to the reading map.

It all started a year ago, I decided to move out of my parents place and with that I felt, lost.

I didn’t know what to do, if I’d get a job or just become anything like a drummer or a singer or whatever, truly.

Politics also stressed me out a lot since political nihilism is on the rise and so was society ignorance towards art and trying to replace it with AI generated bullshit that lacked everything but primarily, a soul, from there I knew we were doomed, in fact I knew that since I knew about Oppenheimer and his creation, the atomic bomb, he basically gave us a power to destroy ourselves and many governments and militaries got seduced by the idea pretty much because they thought it would bring peace, but well as we all know we only exist due to a Soviet called Stanislav Petrov that pretty much prevented a potential nuclear war back in 1983 by identifying a false alarm from a Soviet warning system.

Well, back then we had 2 main extreme ideologies that last until today, fascism and communism, which well, on one hand you let the 1% become much much richer and you will judge minorities, immigration and tell obvious lies that make an excellent propaganda that if you speak against it they’ll get to you and take away everything like in communism, where well, everyone starves and you don’t have a state but their torture method that similarly compares to fascism but they can somehow be much worse, they’ll take away your existence pretty much, they’ll take away your family citizenship alongside yours and they might even kill you in front of them, but well, in the end of the day they’re both dictatorships that don’t work.

Done with this political nonsense and I’d like to address my life that is a complete mess, well, I have no name in this city, they just call me The Traveller, my mental health well, its also not the best and I feel like shit all the time, I’m pretty much a modern day Fernando Pessoa, a Portuguese writer who had multiple so called heteronyms and whose last name translated to English is basically “person”, also he pretty much be considered the man who never lived.

My days are pretty much a mess and I’ll either stay home all day thinking about killing myself or how insignificant we are compared to the whole universe, like, what do you mean the Milky Way is just a void in middle of space and that we just might as well be completely alone on it, with no other living beings and just surrounded by a whole void in space.

I know I also sound like pretty much a modern day nihilist or existentialist and honestly I just won’t deny it due to the fact that I am and yes I just think that we shouldn’t exist by nature’s law and I also just think that nothing truly matters in the end of the day, we are nothing but numbers or just well, I don’t know, accidents?
