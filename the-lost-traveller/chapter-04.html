After I was released from the psych ward I just couldn’t help but get worse and I stopped taking drugs eventually but had much more narrow cuts in my body which well, people started noticing and they just eventually left me so yeah, fuck it, I’ll take much more drugs and be addicted to much more stuff like alcohol and heroin, I mean considering I was in Deutschland I knew I could just take how much alcohol I wanted and I could find heroin and weed pretty much everywhere, I eventually stopped taking heroin and started doing meth but well, I ended up stopping taking such since I already had become numb from taking such and they weren’t doing as much as effect as they did back in the day so I stopped taking them definitely and the max I’d do was smoke some rolling tobacco since I didn’t want to smoke a fucking highway with these stupid branded cigarettes and I decided to just stay more natural and to have less effects on my lungs.

My biggest addiction after that was traveling to forests which felt amazing considering I did it the most on Autumn but then I also had another big addiction that was buying random stuff and consume much much more furniture I didn’t need and meaningless things, with the amount of money I wasted on those I could just buy a whole library and consume much more literature but well, I was as ignorant as the people who didn’t buy George Orwell, Franz Kafka, Pessoa and Camões books so I pretty much just became the stuff I hated the most and I could also just be considered well I don’t know, an hypocrite.

I ended up buying a gun, tried to shoot myself and it didn’t work cuz somehow the gun jammed and religious people called it a miracle and I ended up doing time on a mental hospital that pretty much could be much worse than the psych ward but in the end of the day no matter how much they tried I’d become much worse in theory, in reality I found people who I related to which I actually wish I could be with again since after that I still hadn’t found anyone like them since everyone just felt so empty compared to those beautiful and broken souls.
