Chapter 5 — Blue Lights and Blue Feelings

Read slowly. Use the chapter controls below to continue or return to the reading map.

When I talk about blue feelings, I mostly talk about sadness and numbness, in this case it was both somehow and I couldn’t ask anything except asking for people to stop the noise so I could get some rest but eventually, I started going outside again to nightclubs that most of the time made me feel the same, I’d always drink a coke in those just to have at least some good taste in my mouth, something sweet and not something that was sour like I had all the time.

Even between blue lights I still saw many people dancing and cheering to red books while I’d be there just watching and trying to enjoy the sort of crappy music they just played, shit was not like I thought it would be, I thought it would be like the 70s or 80s nightclubs still but I was dead wrong, they didn’t play bands like Queen, ABBA or the legend himself, David Bowie that made Heroes which well, I’d say Deutsche people love that song as much as I do since there is nothing like it, genuinely.

There was this time I met a guy in a nightclub and he was very nice and I started hanging out with him and we fell in love with each other eventually but it didn’t last long, well we did things couples do, kiss, cuddle, fuck and we also went shopping together but sadly I was a little too much for him so I decided to break up with him and it was hard and I still loved him but I didn’t think he deserved being with a person as unstable as me and he deserved a much better person who didn’t have the same problems as me and as unstable.

The funny thing about all of this is that when I was with him I was actually getting better but at the same time I felt a lot of guilt due to the fact he was very stressed about work and too worried about me to think of work even which well I’d say it’s a sacrifice you do when you’re truly in love with someone but at the same time i’d say that people shouldn’t worry about me at all even my partners.

Personally though, I’d say I’m a terrible partner for some reason.
