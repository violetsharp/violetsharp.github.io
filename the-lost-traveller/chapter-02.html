Chapter 2 — A Quiet Life

Read slowly. Use the chapter controls below to continue or return to the reading map.

For a while, I decided that taking a quiet life would be a better option but I actually found myself in a pretty purple world and well, in case you don’t understand I’ll explain it to you, I basically see a purple world as disorienting in a lot of ways so, what I’m basically saying is I’m completely disoriented in this city and in my head, I’m sick of trying to search for a job which well I shouldn’t according to society’s standard bullshit but in all honesty I just see myself becoming either a writer which I am being right now or an actor in small movies since many people told me I have the talent for it but I don’t know, I don’t even know why I’m searching for a job since I don’t give a shit about money pretty much or anything except for arts or something related to such and as I mentioned before art is dying due to the rise of AI that just copies other people’s work and doesn’t make anything better from it just empty and soulless bullshit.

Back when I was a kid they’d read me Romeo & Juliet and other stories that were made totally by human hand, for example, some Portuguese works from Fernando Pessoa whom I mentioned before and many other Portuguese ones like Luís Vaz De Camões and then other European ones most precisely from the United Kingdom and Czech Republic like George Orwell whose actual name was Eric Arthur Blair and Franz Kafka that wrote masterpieces like The Trial and Metamorphosis while Orwell wrote Animal Farm and 1984 that looking back weren’t so wrong.

One thing that Fernando Pessoa and Luís Vaz De Camões shared with each other was that they died poor and with addictions like alcoholism and well, from that on I’d say people were just becoming ignorant when Pessoa’s books got published with his heteronyms and they wouldn’t either buy them or read them so yeah we should’ve known in those moments our society was doomed.
