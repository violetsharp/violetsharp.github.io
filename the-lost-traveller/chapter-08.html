Chapter 8 — Colors

Read slowly. Use the chapter controls below to continue or return to the reading map.

Right where I was, the mess, it was different this time, I actually thought everything was in its right place but I was dead wrong, I actually started only having 2 colors in my head now, black and white, the world felt much more off this time than it did before, time seemed like it had slowed down, politics was even messier, elections were soon and people kept protesting in the streets for their rights not to be taken and for the 1% to go down.

While people protested and politicians said right things when electioneering, I was just walking past by those like an ignorant and an hypocrite. For some reason I didn’t feel like fighting back against these bullshit politicians and their stupid ideologies but rather just not give a fuck for some reason, and I call myself an hypocrite due to the fact that I pretty much could get my rights taken away in seconds and everything could blow up at any moment.

At that time I was just focusing on whatever, truly, I just wanted to roam around the streets and try to find something interesting but everything just seemed so blatantly boring that I chose to ignore.
