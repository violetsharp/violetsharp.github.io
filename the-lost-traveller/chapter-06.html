Chapter 6 — Void

Read slowly. Use the chapter controls below to continue or return to the reading map.

What the fuck is a void?

Depending on your perspective the void can have many colors and forms, it can be black, white, red or it can be a person, a house or a wall, but in my case, it’s where I accidentally travelled to, I travelled to space and found myself lost there and completely alone, not even stars were around me, just absolute darkness and emptiness which is crazy to me since our universe is so big but so small at the same time with many empty spaces on it as if many pieces of it are just missing or they’re just not supposed to be there.

It’s scary but at the same time I feel much more comfortable believing that and being here, in all fairness though, I feel like an homesick alien and that I shouldn’t be here, but at the same time, I love this feeling and where I am, it’s as if I’m completely alone in the universe and I’m unique, I don’t see myself as a person, cockroach or whatever, I see myself as an entity like an idea which according to a wise man, ideas are bulletproof, you may kill a leader but never the idea.

Even in this void, for some reason I feel like sucking a lemon, everything just seems so sour still about all of this, I’m alone and it feels good but at the same time it’s just so sour and I’m thinking I maybe should go back where I came from, because for some time now I noticed a little light in this void and maybe, just maybe, It’s where I belong.
